01.Abstract

Artificial Intelligence is increasingly being applied in the transportation industry, its power reshapes how people travel, work and interact with modern technology. One of the most significant applications of AI in transportation is autonomous vehicles, particularly self-driving cars. This new technology brings many benefits, including road safety, reduced traffic congestion, and greater convenience for passengers. However, autonomous cars also raise a big concern in liability, privacy, and ethical decision-making in accident situations. By examining the social, ethical, and technological implications of Artificial Intelligence in autonomous vehicles, this project aims to explore the balance between the benefits and concerns in self-driving technology, while analyzing the ethical choices that governments, technology companies, and society must consider when implement autonomous transportation technology in the future.

02.The Why

The reason this was chosen is that it shows how AI has integrated with our daily lives and how it has started to take over many things, such as ways of travel with self-driving cars. This is a unique topic as it shows that this may be the future of how people travel to places and how this will affect society. What could this mean for the future?
